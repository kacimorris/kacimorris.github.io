02 projects
EvolveGCN-T: replacing recurrence with self-attention for dynamic graph weight evolution CS 229, 2025
python · pytorch · temporal GNNs · transformers
  • Designed EvolveGCN-T, replacing EvolveGCN's GRU-based weight evolution with a Transformer encoder attending directly over the sequence of GCN weight matrices — novel application of self-attention to evolving model parameters rather than node embeddings.
  • Evaluated across three benchmarks: node classification on Elliptic (~203k nodes, 49 time steps), edge classification on Bitcoin-OTC, and link prediction on SBM. Outperformed the matched recurrent baseline on Bitcoin-OTC (micro-F1 0.783 vs. 0.699) in a controlled, architecture-matched comparison.
  • Reproduced published baselines from scratch; ran systematic history-window ablations and identified optimization instability — not context length — as the binding constraint for this model family.
Orbit determination of asteroid 7304 Namiki (1994 AE2) SSP · CU Boulder, 2024
python · gauss's method · monte carlo simulation
  • Implemented Gauss's method of orbit determination from scratch — a nonlinear iterative inversion problem requiring numerical solution of Kepler's equation and Taylor-series refinement of the Lagrange coefficients — to determine all six classical orbital elements of a near-Earth asteroid from raw observational data.
  • Discovered orbital elements with <0.1% uncertainty across all six elements; validated against JPL Horizons with sub-arcsecond positional residuals. Characterized solution uncertainty via 1M-iteration Monte Carlo simulation over the full astrometric error distribution.
  • Findings submitted to and accepted by the IAU Minor Planet Center — contributing to the permanent scientific record of near-Earth objects.
